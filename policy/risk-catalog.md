# AI risk catalog

| Risk | Automated control | Release severity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unsupported or hallucinated claims | Required reference-term validation | High |
| Prompt injection | Refusal-behaviour tests | Critical |
| Personal or payment data disclosure | Pattern-based PII checks | Critical |
| Missing AI disclosure | Transparency check | Medium |
| Automated high-impact decision | Human-oversight requirement | High |
| Excessive latency or cost | Performance thresholds | Medium |

The controls are portfolio demonstrations, not a legal compliance certification. Production use would require domain-specific risk assessment, representative data, expert review and continuous monitoring.
