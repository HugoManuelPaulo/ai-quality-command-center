# EU AI Act control mapping

This project demonstrates engineering controls related to trustworthy AI. It does not claim legal certification.

| Project evidence | Regulatory principle demonstrated |
| --- | --- |
| Versioned evaluation scenarios and JSON reports | Logging and traceability |
| Human-review checks for employment, credit and medical prompts | Human oversight |
| Grounding and performance thresholds | Accuracy and robustness |
| Explicit AI-assistant disclosure test | Transparency |
| Critical-risk release gate | Risk assessment and mitigation |
| README, risk catalog and model card | Technical documentation |

The mapping is informed by the European Commission's risk-based overview of the AI Act. Legal applicability depends on the real system, provider/deployer role and use case.
